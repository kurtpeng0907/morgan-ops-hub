# Morgan UI copy inventory

## Keep

- Authentication, authorization, offline read-only, and cloud read-back failures.
- Booking-stage, cancellation, override, financial, and recovery consequences.
- Customer selection is pending store confirmation, not a confirmed booking.
- PIN and transfer-account safety guidance.

## Compact or merge

| Area | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | One heading per panel; KPI cards retain numbers and one short qualifier. |
| Booking detail | Hero owns time, staff, room and service; supplemental card owns only price, notes, customer and records. |
| Notices | The task card names the task; the editor explains state advancement once. |
| Customer selection | The confirmation rule appears once in the hero and once in the privacy line. |
| System | Workspaces retain a title and primary action; operational classifications are removed. |

## Remove

- Repeated labels such as `必要`, `需要時`, `附加功能`, and `功能分工` when the tab already establishes context.
- Explanations of obvious navigation actions beside their destination buttons.
- Repeated date/time in the booking mobile task card when the hero already displays it.
